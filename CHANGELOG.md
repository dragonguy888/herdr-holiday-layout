# Changelog

## 1.00 — 2026-11-18

First release.

- Snowy night background (`night.jpg`), with the sidebar and terminal panes as 50% dark glass.
- `holiday-brand`: Christmas-tree sign with rotating greetings, plus the sidebar footer.
- `holiday-lights`: eight-colour glowing fairy lights. They twinkle out of step, blink fast in
  step when an agent is blocked, and turn off and on with a click.
- `holiday-status`: Christmas and New Year countdown, festive agent pills (click a pill to jump to
  that agent), drifting snow, and the palette hint.
- Theme `holiday`.
